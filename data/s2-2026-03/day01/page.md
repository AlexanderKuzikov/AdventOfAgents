**Day 1 of Google's Advent of Agents — Season 2**

Welcome to **Season 2** of Advent of Agents! A fresh season of AI Agent tutorials blooms this spring.

31 days. 31 tutorials. Production-Ready AI Agents on Google Cloud.

**Every day in March you will:**

-  🌱 Learn one new concept or feature to explore
-  💻 Build an agent with copy-paste commands that actually work
-  📖 Explore links to docs and resources

From advanced agent patterns to multi-agent orchestration. From prototyping to production deployment.

**What's New in Season 2:** Building on the foundations from Season 1, this season goes deeper:

-  [Agent Development Kit](https://google.github.io/adk-docs/?utm_source=adventofagents&utm_medium=markdown&utm_campaign=adventofagents_s2_2026&utm_content=day01): Build agents with the latest ADK features
-  [Vertex AI Agent Engine](https://cloud.google.com/products/agent-builder?utm_source=adventofagents&utm_medium=markdown&utm_campaign=adventofagents_s2_2026&utm_content=day01): Scale your agents to production
-  [Agent Starter Pack](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/agent-starter-pack?utm_source=adventofagents&utm_medium=markdown&utm_campaign=adventofagents_s2_2026&utm_content=day01): E2E production-ready templates
