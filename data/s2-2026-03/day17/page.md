**Summary:** Connect and use Google Workspace connectors in personal Gemini Enterprise agents.

**Day 17 of Google's Advent of Agents — Season 2**

The **Google Workspace** and **Gemini Enterprise** platforms are a natural fit: Google Workspace brings the productivity data & tools, and Gemini Enterprise brings the agents that everyone can build and use in organizations.

Today we showcase:

- How to create your first **Gemini Enterprise application**.
- How to **connect Google Workspace data** to Gemini Enterprise using connectors.
- How to use the Gemini Enterprise **Agent Designer to build personal agents** that rely on Google Workspace connectors.

While the **Agent Designer** offers a powerful, accessible entry point for rapid automation, a **pro-code approach is also supported** for the more complex logic, bespoke API integrations, or advanced data transformations that require the granular control of custom development. But that's a subject for another day!
